1. Too good to be true
2. Fake urgency timer
3. Suspicious link text

01Too good to be true

Real companies rarely give away prizes for doing nothing. Unexpected "you won!" messages are one of the most common phishing hooks.

02Fake urgency

Countdown timers and "act now" language are designed to make you click before you think it through.

03Suspicious link / domain

Look closely at the actual web address, not just the button text. Scam links often use odd, unofficial-looking domains.

04Pressure to click without reading

Legit offers don't need you to rush. If something pushes you to skip reading the details, slow down.
